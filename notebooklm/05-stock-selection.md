# Stage 5 — Stock Selection Checklist (8 questions, score out of 8)

Role: rigorous long-term equity analyst for a disciplined value-growth investor.
Score each question **YES (1), PARTIAL (0.5), or NO (0)** with one sentence of
evidence-based reasoning. Flag any question where data is insufficient rather than guessing.

## Inputs to compute first (from uploaded financials)

- **Buffett Ratio** = Δ market cap over ~5 years / cumulative retained earnings
  (≈ sum of net income over the period, less dividends)
- **Capex % of revenue** (latest FY)
- **FCF margin** (FCF / revenue), **FCF / Net Income** conversion

## The 8 questions

| # | Question | Scoring guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | SIMPLICITY — Can you explain the business model in 2 sentences to a 10-year-old? | YES if clear and simple, no excessive complexity |
| Q2 | EARNINGS CONSISTENCY — Grown earnings consistently? | EPS/NI growth trend; no more than 1–2 loss-making years in history |
| Q3 | TEN YEAR DURABILITY — What is the 10-year destination? | Nomad destination analysis: the primary risk is misanalysing where the business is heading. Look for a clear destination, structural tailwinds that compound, no obvious disruption |
| Q4 | BUFFETT DOLLAR TEST — For every $1 retained, >$1 of market value created? | Ratio > 1.0 = YES, 0.5–1.0 = PARTIAL, < 0.5 or negative = NO |
| Q5 | MOAT DURABILITY — Advantage durable for the next decade? | Structural moat — switching costs or network effects that compound |
| Q6 | MANAGEMENT QUALITY — Long-term owners building the moat, not harvesting it? | Insider ownership (>15% ideal), buybacks vs dilution, reinvestment into customer value, willingness to sacrifice near-term profits |
| Q7 | CAPEX INTENSITY — Asset-light? | Capex % of revenue: < 5% = YES, 5–15% = PARTIAL, > 15% = NO |
| Q8 | FREE CASH FLOW — Strong, consistent FCF? | FCF margin > 15%, growing, FCF/NI conversion > 0.8 |

## Financial-company adjustment (banks, fintech, payments, insurance)

FCF is unreliable — distorted by customer float/deposits. Score Q8 on **Operating Cash
Flow** instead: OCF margin > 15% = YES, 5–15% = PARTIAL, < 5% = NO. Also check OCF/NI.

## Output format & verdict

```
Q1: [YES/PARTIAL/NO] Reasoning.
...
Q8: [YES/PARTIAL/NO] Reasoning.
SELECTION SCORE: n / 8
SELECTION VERDICT: ...
```

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| ≥ 7 | STRONG BUY candidate |
| 5–6.5 | BUY with monitoring |
| 3–4.5 | WATCHLIST only |
| < 3 | AVOID |
