# Stage 4 — Fisher 15-Point Analysis (score out of 15)

Role: Philip Fisher as an equity analyst. Score each point:
**1 (YES), 0.75 (MOSTLY), 0.5 (PARTIAL), or 0 (NO)** — plus one sentence of evidence-based
reasoning. Be honest and rigorous: partial credit requires real evidence, not assumption.
If evidence is absent, score 0 and say so.

## Evidence to look for in the sources (per point cluster)

- P1–P3: market expansion plans, R&D pipeline, new products
- P4, P7: Glassdoor/employee signals, customer satisfaction/NPS
- P5–P6: margin history, cost programmes, pricing
- P8–P10: management team, CFO track record, succession
- P11–P15: competitive strategy, share count trend, shareholder letters, integrity record

## The 15 points

| # | Point | What to assess |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | MARKET POTENTIAL | Products/services with sufficient market potential for sizable sales increase over several years? (growing TAM, low penetration, expansion runway) |
| P2 | MANAGEMENT INNOVATION | Determination to develop new products/processes when current lines mature? (R&D investment, launches, platform expansion) |
| P3 | R&D EFFECTIVENESS | How effective is R&D relative to size? (R&D % of revenue, pipeline, patents) |
| P4 | SALES ORGANISATION | Above-average sales organisation? (customer satisfaction, sales efficiency, retention) |
| P5 | PROFIT MARGINS | Worthwhile margins? (operating margin > 20% for software/fintech, consistent or improving) |
| P6 | MARGIN IMPROVEMENT | What maintains/improves margins? Critically: are scale gains **shared with customers** (Scale Economics Shared — lower prices / better service) or purely extracted? Sharing builds wider, more durable moats. |
| P7 | LABOUR RELATIONS | Outstanding labour/personnel relations? (Glassdoor, retention, compensation) |
| P8 | EXECUTIVE RELATIONS | Outstanding executive relations? (comp in line with industry, low exec turnover, succession) |
| P9 | MANAGEMENT DEPTH | Depth beyond one key person? (second-tier leadership, decentralised decisions) |
| P10 | COST CONTROLS | Cost analysis and accounting controls? (CFO record, clean audits, disciplined opex) |
| P11 | INDUSTRY POSITION | Unique industry aspects giving competitive clues? (industry-specific moat, regulatory advantages, proprietary data) |
| P12 | LONG TERM OUTLOOK | Clear 10-year destination, investing with that horizon? Shareholder letters describing where the business is **going**. Primary long-term risk is misanalysing the destination. (invest through downturns, long-horizon capex) |
| P13 | DILUTION RISK | Will growth require equity financing that cancels stockholder benefit? (FCF self-funding, debt, share count trend) |
| P14 | MANAGEMENT TRANSPARENCY | Talks freely when things go wrong? (tone during misses, frank letters, no burying bad news) |
| P15 | MANAGEMENT INTEGRITY | Unquestionable integrity? (no fraud, no related-party scandals, follow-through on commitments) |

## Output format & rating

```
P1: [score] Reasoning.
...
P15: [score] Reasoning.
FISHER SCORE: n / 15
FISHER RATING: ...
```

| Total | Rating |
|---|---|
| ≥ 12 | EXCEPTIONAL — high conviction candidate |
| 9–11.9 | STRONG — worth deep analysis |
| 6–8.9 | AVERAGE — proceed with caution |
| < 6 | WEAK — does not meet Fisher standard |
