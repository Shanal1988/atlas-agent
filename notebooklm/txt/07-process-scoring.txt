## 7a. Feroldi Quality Score (0–100, Gauntlet can subtract to −54)

Score financial items directly from the uploaded financials (resilience, gross margin,
returns on capital, FCF, EPS — see doc 00 §9 for ranges). Judgement items below; ranges
are hard limits, integer scores.

**Moat & Potential**

```
M1 (0-15) Network effect / product ecosystem: None=0-2, Weak=3-8, Strong=9-15
M2 (0-15) Switching costs: None=0-2, Weak=3-8, Strong=9-15
M3 (0-15) Durable cost advantage (scale, distribution, locations, vertical integration)
M4 (0-15) Intangibles (premium brand, patents, trade secrets, licenses)
M5 (0-10) Counter-positioning (incumbents harmed by copying): None=0, Some=1-5, Strong=6-10
M6 (0-5)  Moat direction: Narrowing=0-1, Stable=2-3, Widening=4-5
P1 (0-7)  Optionality: None=0, Within industry=1-4, Into new industries=5-7
P2 (0-4)  Organic growth runway: ~GDP=0-1, 2-3x GDP=2-3, 15%+ sustained=4
P3 (0-3)  Top dog & first mover in emerging industry / disruptor: No=0, Partly=1-2, Clearly=3
P4 (0-4)  Operating leverage ahead: Negative=0, None=1, Modest=2-3, Tons=4
```
Note: the six Moat raw maxima sum to 75 — scale the Moat subtotal ×30/75 for the /100 score.

**Customers / Company / Management / Stock**

```
C0 (0-5) Customer acquisition cost (S&M % of gross profit): >75%=0-1, 25-75%=2-3, <10% word of mouth=4-5
C1 (0-5) Customer dependence: Highly cyclical=0-1, Moderate=2-3, Recession-proof=4-5
K1 (0-5) Recurring revenue: None=0-1, Some=2-3, Tons=4-5
K2 (0-5) Pricing power: None=0-1, Some=2-3, Tons=4-5
G1 (0-4) Soul in the game: Hired-gun CEO=0-1, Long-tenured=2, Family-run=3, Founder-led=4
G2 (0-3) Mission statement: none/generic=0, simple=1, +inspirational=2, +optionable=3
S1 (0-4) 5-yr vs S&P 500: underperform=0-1, beat=2, +50% over index=3, +100% over index=4
S2 (0-3) Shareholder-friendly actions: none=0, some=1-2, consistent=3
S3 (0-4) Beats expectations: routinely misses=0, mixed=1-2, consistently beats=3-4
```

**The Gauntlet (penalties — each score must be EXACTLY one of the allowed values)**

```
X1  (-10/0)    Accounting irregularities (restatements, auditor changes, SEC inquiries)
X2  (-5/-3/0)  Customer concentration: one >20% rev or AR=-5, one or few >10%=-3, none=0
X3  (-5/-3/0)  Industry disruption: active=-5, plausible=-3, none=0
X4  (-5/-3/0)  Outside forces (commodities, rates, own stock, economy): high=-5, mod=-3, low=0
X5  (-5/-3/0)  Big market loser: >50% vs S&P over 5yrs/IPO=-5, meaningful underperf=-3, no=0
X6  (-5/0)     Binary event pending (patent cliff, legal/regulatory ruling)
X7  (-4/-2/0)  Extreme dilution: >5% annual share growth=-4, 3-5%=-2, <3%=0
X8  (-4/-2/0)  Growth by acquisition: exclusively=-4, partially=-2, organic=0
X9  (-3/0)     Complicated financials
X10 (-3/0)     Antitrust concerns
X11 (-3/-2/0)  HQ country risk: high=-3, medium=-2, low=0
X12 (-2/-1/0)  Currency risk: >75% foreign revenue=-2, >50%=-1, <50%=0
```

**Final = Pre-Gauntlet (/100) + Gauntlet (floor −54).** List items you couldn't score as
data gaps.

## 7c. Ten Vital Signs (0 / 0.5 / 1 each, out of 10)

Score each strictly on evidence; one short sentence each.

```
V1  Strong balance sheet, preferably net cash? (net cash vs net debt, debt trend,
    interest coverage, zero-revenue stress test)  [RULE: if net debt > 0, V1 max 0.5]
V2  Organic revenue growth powered by a large market / long-term tailwinds?
V3  Rising or stable margins, gross and operating? (not growth-at-any-cost)
V4  High/increasing ROIC with growing earnings and FCF? (moat protected, new moats built)
V5  Exceptional CEO and leadership team? (founder, ownership, long-term, culture)
V6  Recurring revenue and/or pricing power?
V7  Medium (or lower) risk profile? (debt, cyclicality, concentration, disruption,
    regulation, earnings quality)
V8  Executing well — strong business momentum?
V9  Mission beyond maximizing profits?
V10 Multi-bagger potential? (innovation, optionality, first mover, saves time/money)
```

Then answer in one sentence each:
- `SANE_VALUATION:` Is the stock selling at a sane valuation?
- `OPPORTUNITY_COST:` What is the opportunity cost vs adding to existing holdings?

## 7e. Investment Stage + Lynch category

**Pre-classify deterministically first**, then pick the exact stage within the window:

| Situation | Candidate window |
|---|---|
| No meaningful revenue | Stages 1–4 |
| Unprofitable, near breakeven (op margin within ±5%) | 7–8 |
| Unprofitable, revenue growth > 20% | 5–6 |
| Unprofitable, modest growth | 4–7 |
| Profitable, growth > 20% | 8–9 |
| Profitable, growth 5–20% | 9–11 |
| Profitable, growth < 5% | 10–12 |

Stage table with **hard position caps**:

| Stage | Label | Do I invest? | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Formation → Product-market fit | No | 0% |
| 5 | Huge revenue growth | <1% | 1% |
| 6 | Reinvestment | <1% | 1% |
| 7 | Margin expansion | 1–2% | 2% |
| 8 | Reach profitability | 2–4% | 4% |
| 9 | Huge profit growth | 2–4% | 4% |
| 10 | Market maturity | 4–10% | 10% |
| 11 | Dividend/buyback | Start to exit | 2% |
| 12 | Dividend aristocrat | Exit | 0% |

Lynch category (pick one): FAST GROWER (20–25% earnings growth) · SLOW GROWER (dividend) ·
STALWART (10–12% growth) · CYCLICAL · TURNAROUND · ASSET PLAY.

Output:
```
STAGE: <number> — <label>  (cap n%)
LYNCH: <category>
REASON: <one sentence>
```
