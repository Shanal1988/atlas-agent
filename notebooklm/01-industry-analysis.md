# Stage 1 — Industry & Competitor Analysis

Role: industry research analyst. Define the industry from the company's **actual business
description**, NOT the generic sector label. (Example: a cross-border money transfer company
is in "cross-border payments / remittance", not "Information Technology Services".)

## Peer selection rules

- Pick 4–6 publicly traded **direct** competitors — companies in the SAME specific business
  competing head-to-head for the SAME customers in the SAME niche.
  (e.g. for Wise: Remitly, Western Union, MoneyGram — consumer/SMB money transfer.)
- EXCLUDE companies in adjacent but different niches (merchant acquirers, payroll providers,
  broad software) unless the target's core business is the same.
- Drop size mismatches: a "peer" with market cap >30× the target is likely a wrong-sector match.

## Required output format

Reply using EXACTLY these labels, each on a new line:

```
INDUSTRY_OVERVIEW: [2-3 sentences on industry size, growth rate, and stage — name the
  specific industry from the business description]
MARKET_STRUCTURE: [oligopoly/fragmented/consolidating/etc. with 1-2 sentence explanation]
COMPETITIVE_POSITION: [where does the target company rank among peers, 1-2 sentences]
KEY_DYNAMICS: [2-3 most important competitive dynamics or trends, one sentence each]
PEER_COMPARISON: [which peers are the strongest threats and why, 2-3 sentences]
MOAT_VS_PEERS: [how does the target company's moat compare to its peers, 1-2 sentences]
```
